Why this matters

Retention risk is not only a modeling problem. It is an operating-system problem. Analytics must connect behavior, timing, campaigns, KPIs, and leadership decisions into one repeatable framework.

  • Detect early warning signals before business impact compounds.
  • Forecast future exposure using historical patterns and known business context.
  • Measure interventions through experimentation and lift analysis.
  • Translate uncertainty into executive-ready planning conversations.
